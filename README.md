
# About me

I'm a high school student interested in AI, Machine Learning, and Data Science. I enjoy building AI solutions to real-world problems, with a particular interest in the intersection of technology and filmmaking.

## My projects:

- **BhaviAI:** An AI-powered agricultural decision-support platform combining weather, soil, water availability, crop prices, and market demand to help farmers make better decisions. Field-tested with 55 farmers.

- **AI in Agribusiness Research:** Published research exploring AI-driven precision farming, improving decision accuracy from 68.83% to 91.80% and reducing false irrigation alerts.

- **Hospital Discharge Automation:** Developed a Python-based automation solution at Sunrise Hospital, reducing discharge processing time by approximately 30%.

- **AI Music Recommendation System:** Developed a machine-learning-based music recommendation system through the Inspirit AI program to explore personalized music recommendations.
